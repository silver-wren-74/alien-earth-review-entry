A one-line review of planet Earth, written by an alien tourist after
two days of field observation. `main.py` writes it to
`/output/entry.txt`:

> Day two: the tall hairless ones are walked twice daily by their
> small furry rulers and carry the rulers' droppings home in bags,
> unpaid, smiling. Five stars. I have applied to be walked.

The outside view: any visitor watching a human follow a dog around,
bag in hand, would conclude the small furry ones give the orders.
The review keeps a tourist's shape — field note, observation, star
rating — and the punch lands last: the reviewer is not invading, it
is applying for the job.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt (1 line, under 200 characters)
```
